# How a lock actually works

## WHAT THIS FILM IS ABOUT
**A key does not turn a lock. It lifts five pins to exactly one height, and then the lock
turns itself.**

Every beat proves that sentence or it is cut.

## ARCHETYPE
Narrated stills. Eight plates, a slow move across each, no cast and no rig. Chosen because
nothing in this film has to *act* — it has to be *seen*, and one clear cutaway holds still
better than any amount of motion.

## THE STRUCTURAL FACT
The **shear line** is one line. Every pin is cut in two somewhere along its length, and the
lock opens only when all five of those cuts sit on that one line at the same moment. If a
plate shows the cuts anywhere else while the plug is turning, the film is teaching something
false — and this is exactly the class of thing a prompt cannot promise.

## SEGMENTS
hook · A key does not turn a lock. It lifts five little pins to exactly one height — and then
the lock turns itself.

0 · Inside every ordinary door lock there is a cylinder within a cylinder. The outer one is
fixed to the door. The inner one — the plug — is the part that turns, and the keyway is the
slot cut through it.

1 · Drilled down through both, in a neat row, are five narrow shafts. Each one holds two
stacked pins and a spring pressing down from above.

2 · With no key in, the springs push every pin down so that it crosses the join between plug
and housing. Five little bridges. The plug cannot rotate, because five pins are nailing it
in place.

3 · The join those pins cross has a name: the shear line. It is one line, all the way along,
and it is the only thing this whole mechanism is about.

4 · Push the key in and each cut along its edge lifts one pin stack by a different amount.
The bitting is not decoration; it is five measurements.

5 · When the key is the right key, every gap between the two pins arrives at the shear line
at once. Not near it. On it.

6 · Nothing is holding the plug now. Turn the key and the plug turns with it, the cam on the
back swings, and the bolt comes clear.

moral · Which is why a copy cut half a millimetre shallow will not open your door. One pin
sitting a hair proud of the shear line is the same as a locked door.

## RIGHTS
Nothing quoted. No trademarks depicted. Generic pin-tumbler mechanism, out of patent for a
century.

## THE FACTS
- Pin tumbler, 5-pin: the ordinary domestic standard. Checked.
- "Shear line" is the correct term for the plug/housing join. Checked.
- Springs push pins DOWN into the plug when no key is present. Checked.
- Key bitting depths correspond to pin stack heights. Checked.
